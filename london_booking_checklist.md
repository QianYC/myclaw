# London Trip — Pre-Departure Booking Checklist

A timeline of what to book and when, to lock in the best prices.

---

## 🗓️ 3 Months Before
- [ ] **Flights** — book when prices dip (Skyscanner price alerts, Google Flights tracking)
- [ ] **Passport check** — ensure valid for at least 6 months past return date
- [ ] **Visa / ETA check** — UK introduced ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation, £10) for many nationalities in 2025. Check at https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-electronic-travel-authorisation-eta
- [ ] **Travel insurance** — compare policies (World Nomads, SafetyWing, or local provider)

## 🗓️ 2 Months Before
- [ ] **Accommodation** — book hotel/Airbnb in Shoreditch or Bethnal Green (free cancellation options where possible)
- [ ] **Tower of London** — pre-book tickets online (£34.80, cheaper than gate)
- [ ] **Any special restaurants** — Dishoom doesn't take reservations for small groups, but note wait times
- [ ] **Day trip transport** — if doing Oxford/Windsor, check off-peak train fares at nationalrail.co.uk

## 🗓️ 1 Month Before
- [ ] **Sky Garden** — book FREE timed entry slots (release ~3 weeks ahead, popular!) at https://skygarden.london
- [ ] **West End show** — decide which show; book via official sites (ATG, LW Theatres) or wait for TKTS same-day deals
- [ ] **eSIM / SIM** — buy Giffgaff / Three / Airalo eSIM (£10–15 for unlimited data)
- [ ] **UK plug adapter** (Type G, 3-pin)
- [ ] **Download apps**: Citymapper, TfL Go, Google Maps (offline area), XE Currency

## 🗓️ 1 Week Before
- [ ] **Notify bank** of travel / enable contactless abroad
- [ ] **Download offline Google Maps** for central London
- [ ] **Check weather forecast** and finalize packing
- [ ] **Print or screenshot**: hotel booking, flight confirmations, ETA, insurance card
- [ ] **Charge** power bank / devices

## 🗓️ Day Before
- [ ] **Online check-in** for flight
- [ ] **Airport transport** — pre-book Heathrow Express, Elizabeth line, or shared shuttle home-side
- [ ] **Cash** — a small amount of GBP (£50–100) for emergencies; most places take card

## 🗓️ Arrival Day in London
- [ ] **Activate eSIM / buy SIM** at airport if not done
- [ ] **From airport to central London:**
  - Heathrow: Elizabeth line (~£12, ~40 min) — cheapest & easiest
  - Gatwick: Thameslink or Gatwick Express (~£10–20)
  - Stansted / Luton: Stansted Express / Thameslink
- [ ] **Contactless** — tap in/out with your phone or card (no Oyster needed anymore)

## 📦 Packing Essentials (UK-Specific)

### Clothing
- [ ] Light rain jacket / packable umbrella (rain is possible any season)
- [ ] Layers (sweater + t-shirts) — London weather changes fast
- [ ] **Comfortable walking shoes** (you'll walk 15k+ steps/day)
- [ ] One smart-casual outfit (for a nicer dinner or theatre)

### Tech
- [ ] UK Type G plug adapter (× 2 ideally)
- [ ] Power bank
- [ ] Phone + charger

### Documents
- [ ] Passport
- [ ] ETA confirmation (digital is fine)
- [ ] Travel insurance details
- [ ] Printed backup of key bookings

### Misc
- [ ] Reusable water bottle (London tap water is excellent + free refills everywhere)
- [ ] Small daypack
- [ ] Basic medications (paracetamol, etc.)
