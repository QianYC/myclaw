# London Trip Plan — 7 Days (Budget, Couple)

## Overview
- **Travelers:** 2 (couple)
- **Duration:** 7 days / 6 nights
- **Budget:** Under £100/day per person (~£1,400 total for two, excluding flights)
- **Style:** Balanced mix — iconic sights, food & pubs, a bit of culture, one day trip
- **Best time to go:** May–Jun or Sep–early Oct (milder weather, fewer crowds, better prices than peak July/August)

---

## Budget Breakdown (per person, per day)

| Category | Typical Spend |
|---|---|
| Accommodation (shared double, budget hotel / Airbnb) | ~£40 |
| Food (café breakfast, cheap lunch, pub/casual dinner) | ~£25–30 |
| Transport (Oyster/contactless, daily cap ~£8.90) | ~£9 |
| Attractions / activities | ~£10–15 |
| Misc / buffer | ~£5–10 |
| **Total** | **~£90–100/day** |

💡 **Money-saving tips:**
- Many top museums are **free** (British Museum, National Gallery, Tate Modern, V&A, Natural History, Science Museum).
- Get an **Oyster card** or use contactless — the daily cap is automatic.
- Book a **London Theatre TKTS booth** same-day ticket for West End shows (£20–40).
- Grab lunch at **Pret/Leon/Wasabi** (~£6–8) or meal-deal supermarkets (Tesco/Sainsbury's ~£4).
- Stay in **Zone 2** (e.g., Shoreditch, Bethnal Green, Bermondsey, Stockwell) — cheaper and still a 15-min tube ride in.

---

## Where to Stay
- **Best budget area:** **Shoreditch / Bethnal Green** (trendy, great food, good Overground/tube links)
- **Alternatives:** King's Cross (central, great rail links), Bermondsey (south-bank, cheaper), Paddington (west, airport-friendly)
- **Types:** Premier Inn, Travelodge, Point A, Hoxton (deals), or Airbnb private double (~£70–90/night for two)

## 7-Day Itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrival & Westminster Classics
- Arrive, drop bags, Oyster/contactless setup
- **Afternoon:** Walk from Westminster → Big Ben → Parliament Square → Westminster Abbey (exterior)
- Stroll along South Bank → London Eye (skip or ride, £30 pp) → Jubilee Gardens
- **Evening:** Pub dinner in Borough/Waterloo — try **The Anchor** or **The George Inn** (historic)

### Day 2 — Royal London
- **Morning:** Buckingham Palace + Changing of the Guard (11:00, free, check schedule)
- Walk through **St. James's Park** and **Green Park**
- **Afternoon:** Trafalgar Square → **National Gallery** (free, 2–3 hrs)
- Covent Garden for street performers + window shopping
- **Evening:** Dinner in Soho (try **Flat Iron** ~£12 steak, or **Bao** for Taiwanese buns)

### Day 3 — Tower & The City
- **Morning:** **Tower of London** (£34.80 — worth it; 3 hrs with Crown Jewels + Beefeater tour)
- Walk over **Tower Bridge**
- **Afternoon:** Sky Garden (**free**, book ahead for panoramic views) OR Monument climb (£6)
- **Evening:** **Borough Market** for dinner — global street food, £10–15 pp

### Day 4 — Museums + South Kensington
- **Morning:** **Natural History Museum** (free)
- **Afternoon:** **V&A Museum** (free) OR **Science Museum** (free) — pick one
- Walk through **Hyde Park** → Serpentine
- **Evening:** Pub in Notting Hill or dinner in Chelsea; consider a West End show (TKTS booth same-day, ~£25–40)

### Day 5 — East London & Markets
- **Morning:** **Columbia Road Flower Market** (Sundays only) or **Brick Lane Market** (Sundays)
- Explore **Shoreditch** street art — free walking tour or self-guided (look up "Shoreditch street art map")
- **Afternoon:** Bagel at **Beigel Bake** (£2!), curry on Brick Lane for dinner (~£12)
- **Evening:** Craft beer at **BrewDog** or rooftop bar at **Queen of Hoxton**

### Day 6 — Day Trip (pick one)
**Option A — Oxford** (1 hr by train, ~£25 return off-peak)
- Wander colleges, Radcliffe Camera, Bodleian Library, lunch at **The Eagle and Child**
**Option B — Windsor & Eton** (30 min by train, ~£12)
- Windsor Castle (£30), riverside walk, quaint town
**Option C — Greenwich** (in London; cheap!)
- Cutty Sark, Royal Observatory (Prime Meridian), Greenwich Market, river boat back (~£10)

👉 **Recommended for budget:** Greenwich — cheapest & very rewarding.

### Day 7 — British Museum & Last Bites
- **Morning:** **British Museum** (free, 3 hrs — Rosetta Stone, Elgin Marbles, mummies)
- **Lunch:** Afternoon tea done cheap — **Bea's of Bloomsbury** (~£25) or a classic pub Sunday roast (~£15)
- **Afternoon:** Camden Town — Camden Market, walk along Regent's Canal to King's Cross
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner — Dishoom (book ahead, ~£20 pp, iconic)

---

## Packing & Practical Tips
- 🌧️ **Weather:** Always pack a light rain jacket + layers, even in summer
- 👟 **Shoes:** Comfortable walking shoes — you'll walk 15k+ steps/day
- 🔌 **Plug:** UK Type G (3-pin), bring adapter
- 📱 **SIM / eSIM:** Giffgaff or Three £10 for unlimited data
- 💳 **Payment:** Contactless works everywhere (incl. tubes/buses)
- ⏰ **Pub kitchens** often close by 21:00–22:00 — don't dine too late
